# Meta audience and placement opportunities

Read [the analysis principles](../references/ads-analysis-principles.md),
[response pattern](../references/workflow-response-pattern.md), and
[Meta reference](../manage-ads/references/meta-ads.md).

Resolve the brand, Meta connection, campaign scope and complete dates. Inspect the
campaign objective and configured ad-set targeting before interpreting delivered
performance. Intended targeting and actual delivery answer different questions.

Use separate supported breakdown reads for platform/placement, device,
age/gender, country, region, hourly delivery and frequency. Do not combine
incompatible dimensions, average rates across rows, sum reach, or assume an
unknown bucket is a targetable audience. Follow every required page and keep the
same filters, attribution and conversion event across comparisons.

Judge segments by objective-aligned outcomes and enough delivery to be useful.
Separate:

- proven efficient segments with meaningful volume;
- expensive segments with enough evidence for a bounded test;
- under-delivered segments that are unknown rather than poor;
- saturation signals where frequency rises while incremental reach or response
  weakens.

Do not turn one winning demographic into a stereotype or recommend broad
exclusions from a small sample. Consider whether creative, placement format,
budget allocation or Meta's delivery optimization explains the mix before
blaming audience quality. Prefer one controlled change at a time and state what
metric should confirm it.

Return the few dimensions that materially change the decision, with spend,
results and a short why. Propose a prioritized audience/placement test plan and
name what should remain unchanged. This analysis changes nothing: offer a prepared
change and measurement plan. Apply supported country, age or feed-placement edits
only through the [manage-ads workflow](../manage-ads/SKILL.md) after approval;
never claim targeting was updated unless that workflow confirms it.
